# Privacy Policy

Worth It? is a Chrome extension that shows prices as hours of your work.

## What it handles

- **Pay information you enter:** pay amount, pay period, hours worked, tax setting, currency, and any exchange rates you type in.
- **Prices on pages you visit:** the extension reads price text on web pages in your browser to calculate how many hours an item costs.

## Where it goes

All of this stays on your device. Settings are saved in Chrome's local extension storage. Prices are read and converted in your browser and are not stored.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

## Optional exchange-rate sync

If you turn on "Sync once" or "Daily" exchange rates, the extension requests public reference rates from frankfurter.dev over HTTPS. That request contains only your currency code (for example "USD"). Like any web request, it also exposes your IP address to that service. Your pay, the pages you visit, and prices are never sent. Sync is off by default, and switching back to "Manual" stops it.

## Deleting your data

Remove the extension, or clear its data from `chrome://extensions`. All stored settings are deleted.

## Limited Use

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose of showing prices as time worked. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by any person.
